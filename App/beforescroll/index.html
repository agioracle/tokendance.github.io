BeforeScroll · iPhone

Earn your time.
Enjoy your scroll.

Turn movement, reading and focus into minutes in your time wallet. Choose the apps you want to protect, then use your earned time with intention.

Available features include steps, walking and running distance, active energy, reading monitoring, focus sessions with nature sounds, a time ledger and Screen Time app protection. English and Simplified Chinese. Light and dark appearance.

Actual app screens with fictional demonstration data.

Membership, clearly explained.

An active monthly or annual subscription, including an eligible free trial, is required to use or borrow time. Paid membership also enables custom exchange rates, earning limits and borrowing amounts; trial users use the default rules. The monthly plan offers a 7-day free trial to eligible users. The annual plan has no trial. Apple shows current localized prices and eligibility before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal; manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings.

Your data stays on your device.

Health, Screen Time, wallet and focus records are processed on device. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Apple handles purchases. No TokenDance account is required. Read the full privacy policy.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Actual-usage allowances and reading monitoring require iOS 17.4 or later and Screen Time authorization. Health-based rewards require authorized Apple Health data. Selected apps share one cumulative allowance. Daily wallet balance and allowances reset.
